Privacy Policy: LearnAIoffline

Last updated: 21 November 2026

LearnAIoffline ("the app") is an offline learning app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and it does not use the internet or ask for any permissions.

Information stored on your device

To show your progress, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • which lessons you opened and completed, and when;
  • your answers to practice questions (correct or incorrect, number of attempts and last choice);
  • bookmarks you create;
  • daily study activity (time spent and number of questions answered per day);
  • app settings such as theme, colour theme, daily goal, reading text size and code text size.

This information never leaves your device through the app. The developer cannot see it.

Android backup

If you have switched on Android's device backup for your Google account, Android may include the app's local data in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is managed by Android and your Google account settings, not by the app, and the developer never receives the data.

Exporting and importing progress

In Settings › Data you can export your progress to a backup file or import one. The file is saved only where you choose (for example your Downloads folder) using Android's file picker. The app never uploads it anywhere.

Links

The app shows a "Developed by Sj.Pixels" link. Tapping it opens sjpixels.com in your web browser; the app itself sends no data. Websites you visit have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can delete all stored data at any time with Settings › Reset all progress, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is a general-audience educational tool for software developers and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
